Classic Pancakes

These fluffy homemade pancakes are quick, easy to make, and perfect for breakfast. Serve them with butter, syrup, or your favorite fruit toppings.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk together the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until combined.
  4. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat and pour the batter onto the pan.
  5. Cook until bubbles form, flip, and cook until golden brown.
  6. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.
